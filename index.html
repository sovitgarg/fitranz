Common Questions

Do I need gym access to work with you?

Not necessarily. While gym access allows for more variety, I can design effective home workouts with minimal equipment (resistance bands, dumbbells). We'll discuss your setup during the consultation.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients notice changes in energy and strength within 2-3 weeks. Visible body composition changes typically appear around 4-6 weeks with consistent effort. Significant transformations happen over 12+ weeks.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

I work with all dietary preferences — vegetarian, vegan, eggetarian, or non-veg. Allergies and intolerances are factored into your meal plan. The goal is to make nutrition sustainable for YOUR lifestyle.

How much time do I need to commit?

Most programs require 4-6 hours per week for training (45-60 min sessions, 4-5 days). Nutrition doesn't require extra time — just smarter choices. I design programs around busy schedules.

What makes you different from other coaches?

I've competed at the highest level and won. I don't just teach theory — I've applied these principles to my own body under the most demanding conditions. Plus, I limit my client roster to ensure quality coaching.

Can I get a refund if it doesn't work?

I stand behind my coaching. If you follow the program consistently for 30 days and don't see progress, we'll work together to troubleshoot. I'm invested in your success because your results are my reputation.
